Released 2026-10-24.

Eyefinder 0.3 adds the **2D games** (GTA1, London 1969, London 1961, GTA2 and GTA Advance) on every platform, a **release Index**, a **Documentation** page, and tells **executable builds and stores** apart.

## New
* **The 2D games:**
  * **GTA1, London 1969 and London 1961 (PC):**
    * SDT/RAW sound banks, cutscene speech and music WAVs, CD audio tracks;
    * GRY / G24 styles (tiles, variants, sprites, stored deltas), FON images;
    * FXT text.
  * **GTA2 (PC):**
    * SDT/RAW banks, including the police radio words;
    * voice clips and music WAVs;
    * STY styles, frontend TGA images;
    * GXT text.
  * **GTA2 (Dreamcast):** STR music streams (Yamaha AICA ADPCM), plus STY styles and GXT text.
  * **PlayStation, Game Boy Color and Game Boy Advance:** the releases are identified. On PlayStation, GTA1 and London 1969 also play their CD audio tracks.
* **Disc images:** BIN/CUE, ISO, GDI, DiscJuggler CDI and POPStarter VCD. PC discs are identified against redump by their track sizes and data-track SHA-1.
* **Release Index:** 399 releases of 17 GTA games, from pinned redump and No-Intro DATs, redump's PC DAT, titledb and Wikipedia. Console copies are named from it by serial, Dreamcast IP.BIN or ROM hash. The gaps the sources leave are listed, not filled in.
* **Documentation page:** every file type of every game and platform, each with its status, sources and real-file confirmations. [MISSING.md](https://github.com/galaxygas/Eyefinder/blob/main/MISSING.md) is generated from the same data.
* **Versions and stores are told apart (#3):** the executable build and the store (Steam, Rockstar Games Launcher, retail) are identified separately.
* **Adding games:** one Add dialog that detects in the background and shows what it found. Mixed loose files keep their audio.
* **Library:** grouped by game in release order, with compact rows.
* **Themes menu:** Dark, Light, System and **Vice City Night**.
* **Command line:** `files` (the file list of a folder or image) and `docs missing`.
* **Fixes:**
  * playback speed of a track after one with another sample rate;
  * asset table resizing (#1);
  * the edition is kept in export reports.

Detection of the GTA2 PC 2004 release and of the GTA1 PC disc is verified on the maintainer's copies. The 2D decoders are checked against vgmstream, ffmpeg and the format documents on synthetic and real files, but they stay "from spec" until a listening check. Details and sources: [docs/SUPPORT.md](https://github.com/galaxygas/Eyefinder/blob/main/docs/SUPPORT.md) and [docs/FORMATS.md](https://github.com/galaxygas/Eyefinder/blob/main/docs/FORMATS.md).

If you own one of these games, a [format report](https://github.com/galaxygas/Eyefinder/blob/main/CONTRIBUTING.md#reporting-a-format-result) helps move a format to Verified.
